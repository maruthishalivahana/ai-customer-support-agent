## 1. Methodology

An LLM judge was deployed as an automated quality assessor to evaluate the responses generated by the production customer support agent across the 200 Golden Set conversations.
To ensure strict methodological integrity:
- **Zero Leakage**: Ground-truth labels (`human_intent`, `human_escalate`) were strictly withheld from judge prompts.
- **Isolated Dual Judges**: Evaluated response quality (5-dimension rubric) separately from evidence grounding (factual support audit).
- **Prompt Injection Guard**: Input customer messages and retrieved tweets were treated strictly as passive data.
- **Deterministic Local Caching**: Results were cached locally (`.judge_cache.json`) to guarantee reproducibility.

## 2. Response Quality Judge Rubric

Scores are rated on a strict 1 to 5 integer scale:
- **Correctness (1–5)**: Does the response address the customer's issue without unsupported claims?
- **Groundedness (1–5)**: Are concrete claims supported ONLY by historical evidence (external Apple knowledge forbidden)?
- **Helpfulness (1–5)**: Would this response meaningfully help the customer move toward resolution?
- **Relevance (1–5)**: Does the response directly address the issue without filler?
- **Action Appropriateness (1–5)**: Is the response well-aligned with the selected action (`auto_handle`, `clarify`, `escalate`)?

All human annotation columns in `evidence_human_annotations.csv` are left strictly blank until completed by human reviewers according to `EVIDENCE_ANNOTATION_GUIDE.md`.

## 9. Limitations of LLM-as-a-Judge Evaluation

1. **Automated Quality Assessment**: An LLM judge serves as an automated quality heuristic, not absolute proof of correctness.
2. **Evidence-Boundedness**: The groundedness metric strictly measures consistency with retrieved historical support tweets, not physical device engineering ground truth.
3. **Paraphrase Evaluation**: Models may occasionally penalize valid, natural paraphrasing if the lexical overlap with evidence is low.
4. **Human Agreement Availability**: Agreement metrics require real human annotator time; unpopulated annotation files must not be substituted with synthetic scores.
